I am a Postdoctoral Fellow in the Department of Computer Science at **Boston College**, working with [Prof. Usman A. Khan](https://www.eecs.tufts.edu/~khan/). My research sits at the intersection of **robotics, control theory, and combinatorial optimization**, with a focus on developing scalable algorithms with rigorous performance guarantees.

**Research themes:**

- **Graph-SLAM & Active Perception:** streaming loop-closure selection, online camera scheduling, landmark anchoring for map maintenance
- **Sensor Scheduling:** multi-agent scheduling for large sensor networks, real-time algorithms for time-varying systems, budgeted tactile measurement selection
- **Learning & Optimization:** data selection for foundation model training, curvature-aware client selection for federated learning
- **Spectral & Graph Methods:** graph connectivity maximization, structured inference and decision-making

I completed my **Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering** at Northeastern University (GPA: 4.0/4.0) and hold M.Sc. degrees in Mathematics (Montclair State University, GPA: 4.0/4.0) and Electrical Engineering (University of Kerman). My work has been published in venues including *IEEE Transactions on Robotics*, *IEEE Transactions on Automatic Control*, and *Automatica*, and presented at top venues including ICRA, IROS, CDC, and ACC. I have been invited to give talks at Oxford, EPFL, UPenn, University of Waterloo, and the INFORMS Optimization Society Conference.
